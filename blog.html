The AI Onboarding Agent:
Your New Intern Never Sleeps

Here's where CloudTentO gets genuinely interesting. They built an AI Onboarding Agent that reads unstructured text — a voice note transcript, a forwarded WhatsApp message, even a scribbled email — and pulls out vendor details like a trained data entry specialist.

Paste This

"Hi, I'm Sri Lakshmi Tent House in Beeramguda. We have 10 canopies, 500 chairs, sound systems. Contact 9988776655. Specialising in weddings and corporate events."

The AI extracts everything automatically

The AI extracts business name, phone, location, inventory items with quantities, specialisations, and even assigns a quality score. If the score is above 60%, the vendor is auto-approved. Below that, it flags missing fields for human review.

For a market where most tent house owners don't have websites, don't use CRMs, and certainly don't fill out multi-page registration forms, this is transformational. The AI meets them where they already are: in messy, human text.

The Secret Weapons: SOPs & Inventory Intelligence

Most marketplaces stop at "here's a list of vendors." CloudTentO goes further — it actually teaches both sides how to do their jobs better.

The platform hosts detailed SOPs for six event types: Weddings, Birthdays, Corporate Conferences, Political Meetings, Temple Festivals, and School Annual Days. Each covers pre-event planning, role assignments, on-site setup, safety protocols, and teardown procedures.

Then there's the Inventory Guide & Calculator — input your guest count and it tells you exactly how many chairs (always 110% of guests — because late arrivals are real), tables, lights, and labourers you need. Plus a Master Inventory Checklist that small business owners can use to audit their godown stock before the busy season.

Why Hyderabad? Why Now?

Hyderabad's booming event economy spans Hitech City corporate offsites to Old City traditional weddings.

Hyderabad's event economy is exploding. From Hitech City corporate offsites to traditional weddings in Old City, from political rallies in Rangareddy to temple festivals in Medchal — the demand is diverse, frequent, and high-value.

But the supply side is hyper-local and deeply informal. Most tent houses are family businesses with no digital presence. They rely on word-of-mouth, local notice boards, and the occasional Justdial listing that hasn't been updated since 2019.

CloudTentO's insight is simple: Don't try to digitise the vendor. Digitise the connection between vendor and customer.

The Business Model: Refreshingly Honest

CloudTentO doesn't hide its economics. Direct calls: 0% commission. Managed bookings: platform fee applies. This transparency builds trust. Vendors aren't suspicious of a platform eating their margins. Customers aren't being stealth-marked up.
